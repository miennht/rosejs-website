## Approved industries

RoseJS serves **multiple industries**. Healthcare and healthcare insurance are primary specializations; **eCommerce** is an explicit secondary focus. RoseJS does **not** serve healthcare only.

### Healthcare technology

Digital health products, clinical and operational platforms, and engineering organizations modernizing regulated or operationally complex systems.

### Healthcare insurance and payer platforms

Health plans, payer operations, claims, enrollment, billing, RCM workflows, clearinghouse and partner integrations, and batch/real-time processing constraints.

### eCommerce

eCommerce platform modernization, integration strategy, technical debt reduction, and AI-first engineering practices for retail and digital commerce teams.
